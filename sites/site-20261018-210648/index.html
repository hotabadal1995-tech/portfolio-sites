The Cozy Vibe

Located in the heart of Pune, Chai pe Charcha isn't just a cafe; it's an emotion. Inspired by the traditional "Irani Cafe" culture with a modern rustic twist, we offer a sanctuary from the city's hustle.


Whether you're looking for a quiet spot to read or a lively corner for "charcha" with friends, our wooden decor and warm yellow lights create the perfect ambiance.
