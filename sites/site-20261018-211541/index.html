Our Story

Located in the bustling heart of Pune, Chai pe charcha was born from a simple love for traditional brewing and meaningful conversations. We believe that the best ideas are born over a steaming "cutting" chai.


Open daily from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, we provide a cozy corner for students, professionals, and dreamers alike.

The Favorites

Signature Masala Chai

Our secret blend of cardamom, ginger, and hand-picked tea leaves from Assam.
